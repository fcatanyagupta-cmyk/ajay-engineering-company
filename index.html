Our story

Nearly five decades on Aishbagh Road, Lucknow.

Founded in 1977, Ajay Engineering Company is a leading manufacturer of scaffolding products and shuttering material, operating pan-India with a primary focus on Uttar Pradesh. Our products are made with quality raw material to industry standards — known for durability, low maintenance and corrosion resistance — and are available in a wide range of specifications.

FAQ

Yes. Our primary market is Uttar Pradesh, but we dispatch by road to project sites across India. Freight is quoted along with your order.

What payment modes do you accept?

Cash, cheque and demand draft. A GST invoice is issued for every order.

Do you take small orders?

Yes. Along with bulk and wholesale orders, our Aishbagh Road store serves retail buyers too.
